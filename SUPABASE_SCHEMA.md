# Supabase Schema - MarketFlow AI

## Goal

Define the initial database schema for MarketFlow AI.

The database must store products, customer questions, AI-suggested answers, human review, shipping zones and marketplace integrations.

> **Note:** This is the target (normalized, English-named) schema. The tables currently live in Supabase use the Spanish names described in `DATA_STRUCTURE.md`.

## Initial scope

Initial MVP:

- Country: Colombia.
- Initial marketplace: Mercado Libre.
- Automation: suggested, not automatic.
- Human review: mandatory before publishing.

## Future scalability

The structure must support:

- More Colombian cities.
- Other Latin American countries.
- eBay integration.
- Shopify integration.
- Partial or full automation in later phases.

## Initial tables

### 1. products

Stores product information.

Suggested fields:

- id.
- marketplace.
- marketplace_product_id.
- title.
- description.
- price.
- currency.
- stock.
- country.
- city.
- condition.
- warranty.
- status.
- created_at.
- updated_at.

### 2. shipping_zones

Stores shipping costs and times by country, department, city or zone.

Suggested fields:

- id.
- product_id.
- country.
- department.
- city.
- shipping_cost.
- delivery_time_min_days.
- delivery_time_max_days.
- is_available.
- created_at.
- updated_at.

### 3. customer_questions

Stores questions received from marketplaces.

Suggested fields:

- id.
- marketplace.
- marketplace_question_id.
- marketplace_product_id.
- product_id.
- customer_id.
- question_text.
- customer_country.
- customer_city.
- status.
- received_at.
- created_at.

### 4. ai_suggested_answers

Stores AI-generated answers.

Suggested fields:

- id.
- question_id.
- product_id.
- suggested_answer.
- model_provider.
- model_name.
- confidence_score.
- risk_level.
- needs_human_review.
- created_at.

### 5. human_reviews

Stores the human decision on an answer.

Suggested fields:

- id.
- suggested_answer_id.
- question_id.
- reviewer_decision.
- final_answer.
- reviewer_notes.
- reviewed_at.

Possible values for reviewer_decision:

- approved.
- edited.
- rejected.
- needs_more_info.

### 6. marketplace_integrations

Stores general integration info per marketplace.

Suggested fields:

- id.
- marketplace.
- country.
- seller_id.
- account_name.
- status.
- created_at.
- updated_at.

Note: real tokens shouldn't be stored in plain text unless necessary. They should preferably be handled with environment variables or a secure secrets system.

### 7. automation_rules

Stores rules for future automation.

Suggested fields:

- id.
- rule_name.
- marketplace.
- country.
- question_type.
- allow_auto_reply.
- min_confidence_score.
- requires_human_review.
- is_active.
- created_at.
- updated_at.

## Main flow relationship

1. A product is stored in `products`.
2. A question arrives from Mercado Libre and is stored in `customer_questions`.
3. The AI generates an answer, stored in `ai_suggested_answers`.
4. The user reviews the answer and that decision is stored in `human_reviews`.
5. If approved, the answer can be published on the marketplace.

## Data coming from Mercado Libre

- marketplace_question_id.
- marketplace_product_id.
- customer_id.
- question_text.
- received_at.
- seller_id.
- product title.
- product price.
- product condition.
- product status.

## Data entered manually

- Shipping rules.
- Return policies.
- Warranties.
- Internal seller notes.
- Automation rules.
- Restrictions by city or country.

## Data generated by the AI

- Suggested answer.
- Confidence level.
- Question type.
- Risk level.
- Human review recommendation.

## Human review

In the MVP, every answer must go through human review.

The AI must not publish answers directly on Mercado Libre.

## Future automation

In later phases, some answers could be automated if they meet conditions such as:

- Low-risk question.
- Information available in the database.
- High confidence.
- No price changes.
- No complaints.
- No sensitive promises.

## Security

- Do not store real keys in `.md` files.
- Do not push `.env` to GitHub.
- Use `.env.example` only as a template.
- Store sensitive tokens in environment variables or secrets.
- Enable security rules (RLS) before production.
